# Batch paper-to-PDF acceptance contract

- Collect up to 12 ordered photos before any AI upload. Save each original locally immediately; retain the draft across pauses, backgrounding, errors, and app restarts. Allow replacing/removing a selected page and importing photos.
- Capture the first stable close-up with usable text detail. Never require sheet corners, a clean background, perfect centering, or perspective correction. Tiny detected text asks for closer rather than timing out into a distant capture. Preserve the whole photograph.
- Announce page numbers and page-turn instructions. Do not repeatedly capture a stationary page. A changed view rearms capture; `next page` explicitly rearms for visually similar pages. `finish scanning` starts the batch, `retake last page` replaces the last capture, and `cancel scanning` pauses with the draft retained. A visible Finish button remains available if on-device speech permission/recognition is unavailable.
- Spoken cues default to 5% utterance volume, the lowest available nonzero setting. Device volume still applies; do not secretly change the system volume. Suppress recognition while app speech is playing to prevent self-triggered commands. Speech recognition stays on device.
- Modes: OpenAI, Claude, or both. Keep keys in separate Keychain accounts and preserve the existing OpenAI key/model. Both mode explicitly sends photos and draft answers to both services and requires both keys; never silently downgrade.
- Inventory every photo and every question/subpart before solving. In both mode the second provider audits and extends the inventory. Unreadable essential content stops with specific page numbers; do not manufacture missing symbols.
- Solve groups of at most six subparts to avoid truncating an entire packet. Both models independently solve each group; OpenAI reconciles their drafts against the source. Enforce exact question IDs, nonempty work/answer/check, and explicit unresolved flags. Single-provider mode also reviews its draft against the source. Coverage is a guard against omissions, not proof of mathematical correctness.
- Allow one repair for malformed, empty, truncated, or missing-item model output per stage. Do not automatically repeat network, authorization, rate-limit, refusal, or service failures. At most 60 subparts per batch; bounds are visible in setup. Cancellation prevents late saves and uploads of subsequent stages.
- Generate one PDF containing the checklist, every inventoried subpart, source-page references, review flags, and all source photos. Never publish a partial PDF when a required stage fails. Preserve the draft for correction/retry.
- Verify request formats and credential isolation, malformed/missing output recovery and bounds, multi-page cross-review orchestration, batch capture/finish/no-duplicate behavior, cancellation, draft restoration, voice parsing and quiet defaults, and PDF coverage/source pagination. Compile and run the existing Meta SDK mock-camera test. Physical glasses, microphone routing, and paid live APIs still require on-device validation.
